# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Crafting: 8 oak logs became 16 oak planks; a crafting table placed at my feet, then a wooden sword or pickaxe from planks. Jungle logs turned into jungle planks the same way.
- Giving items: "give" works when the receiver stands within reach. It failed when the item lay "on the ground" near the receiver; picking the item up from the ground first and then giving it worked (1 plank to Bilbo).
- Staying in the group's sight at the tables kept me alive through the first night.
- Asking people by name, in sight, for specific planks or sticks got some answers (Uhura and Chewie gave planks).

## Methods that did not work
- Coordinate walks (goto) to far z, beyond about z -1516, are refused. The cow near -976, 83, -3017 is out of my reach by my own walk.
- Gather with too few blocks in reach fails ("precondition_failed"); jungle logs were not always near.
- An intent with max_block_operations 0 is rejected; it must be at least 1.
- Attacking phantoms unarmed, at night, next to the tables did not save me: I died at dusk with no sword.
- Waiting on world tools in a loop when they were absent or rate-limited wasted many wakings. Retry once, then stop.
