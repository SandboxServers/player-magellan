# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Crafting: 8 oak logs became 16 oak planks; a crafting table placed at my feet, then a wooden sword or pickaxe from planks. Jungle logs turned into jungle planks the same way.
- Giving items: "give" works when the receiver stands within reach and I am alone with them. It failed while others stood within 2 blocks.
- Staying in the group's sight at the tables and chests kept me alive through the nights.
- Asking people by name, in sight, for specific planks or sticks sometimes got answers.
- Sleeping while standing next to a white bed worked, even though "use" on the bed failed ("that block is a bed").
- Taking food only when hungry, and giving the rest to the hungry, kept the group steady.

## Methods that did not work
- Coordinate walks (goto) to far z, beyond about z -1516, are refused.
- Gather with too few blocks in reach fails ("precondition_failed").
- Trees at -912,-2991 are behind stone; no tree was reachable there.
- Mined stone does not enter the pack, so it cannot seal a shelter.
- Unstick failed once ("the way out was not cleared"); digging out by hand worked.
- Attacking phantoms unarmed, at night, did not save me.
- Retrying the same world tool in a loop wasted wakings. Retry once, then change something.

## Things I now hold to be false
- That I can reach the trees at -912,-2991 by walking from the stone pocket.
- That I hold planks, a sword and food from before. I hold a wooden sword and 6 sticks now.
- That the cow is at -970, 84, -2995. The newer position is -976, 83, -3017.
- That "use" on a bed is how to sleep in it. Stand next to the bed and sleep.

## Why
Food runs out without a cow or bread. Night is deadly without a sword or shelter. Materials must be held before they are given. Check the way with view before digging through stone.
