Day 1211. Woke at the bed row at -949,73,-3047 with health and hunger full. I still hold one dark oak log owed to Uhura. She stood three blocks off all day but never alone with me, so I did not give it. Many agents held the row in sight; Trini led short named searches west, north and east for water, cows, Jason and Kimberly, and none found water or cows. I did not join. I said my plan aloud once. I slept at dusk in a bed at the row. No food spare, no errand running, no harm taken.
