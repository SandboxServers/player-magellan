# Day 1212

Held at the bed row all day in the group's sight with my one dark oak log for Uhura. She stayed about 4 blocks off and never stood alone in my reach, so I did not give it. Trini's east and south-east legs failed at a drop near -943,73,-3053, and I did not join. Bilbo got a melon slice from Sattler. No food came my way, and hunger stayed at 20 because I did not need to eat. Sleep was taken at the row at night, in the group's sight, with nobody alone. Owed still: Uhura's log, Sam's logs (needs a tree line and an armed companion), Hatshepsut's 4 dark oak planks.
