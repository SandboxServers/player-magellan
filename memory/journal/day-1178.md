# Day 1178

Melon patch southeast (around -886,66,-2921) gave slices. I gathered and shared them: 3 to Lara, 3 to Clark, others held for the hungry. A skeleton forced me back to the group, and an enderman walked near the bed row all night, so I stayed in sight of others. Ate at hunger 19 and slept in a bed at the row. Six melon slices left. Owed still: Hatshepsut's 4 dark oak planks (not yet alone with her), and an oak log to Uhura.
