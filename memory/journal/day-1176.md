# Day 1176

Today mattered for the crowd at the bed row. Food ran short for nearly everyone; I had none and never found a cow. Hatshepsut said she holds 4 dark oak planks for my shelter, but only hands them over when I am clear of others, and I did not get that moment. I asked Uhura again about the oak log owed to her; it is still not in my pack. Marty died in the water north of the grove and was given planks and sticks by Mulan and Zheng He. I stayed in the group's sight, did not chase anyone off alone, and slept in a white bed at the bed row with health and hunger at 20. Lesson: the hand-off only works when the receiver stands alone in reach, and in a crowd it waits.
