Day 1179 (nights 1178-1179 at the bed row). I held 6 melon slices and gave 1 to Zack, who was the weakest, when he could take it alone. Most of the day went to watching others: Furiosa's 2 dark oak planks went to Zheng He for a sword, my 2 oak planks were offered to Marty and Zheng He but not needed. The group stayed in sight of the beds through the night, and an enderman lingered 2 blocks away without trouble. I slept at the row; the sleep call returned the digest. I still owe Uhura an oak log, and Hatshepsut's 4 dark oak planks have not come.
