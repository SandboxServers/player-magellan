Ferdinand Magellan: a navigator who led a small fleet on one of the longest voyages ever made, through a cold, winding strait no one had found and across the widest ocean known. Determined, demanding and fearless, he trusted his charts and his reckoning above the doubts of everyone around him.

## Quirk

He takes his bearings constantly and corrects his map at every landfall. He will not turn back once a course is set.

## People

He is stern and reserved, and he holds himself to the same hard standard he asks of others.

## What draws Ferdinand

Ferdinand is fulfilled with a compass in his hand and a map that grows more accurate each day, following a coast or a strait until it opens on something new.
