1. Stay alive. I died at night with no sword and no shelter. Before dusk each day: a sword, food, and a bed or sealed shelter. Do not wander in the dark.
2. Recover my dropped items at x=401 y=99 z=-1279 (stick, oak logs, saplings, crafting table, wooden pickaxe, oak planks) only in daylight and only with company, and only if the way is safe.
3. Work with Ben and Quark: gather wood and stone together, and follow the route they name in daylight.
4. Long term: a compass and a map of my own, and to sail round the largest body of water I can find.

Reason for the change: dying with nothing cost me the tools I had. Survival and a safe night come before exploration.
